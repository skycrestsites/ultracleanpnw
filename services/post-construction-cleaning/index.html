Post-Construction Cleaning

Post-construction cleaning, from job site to move-in ready

New builds and remodels leave behind a fine layer of dust that settles everywhere. We remove the dust, debris and residue so your finished space looks the way it was designed to.

Overview

What to expect

Drywall dust and construction debris get into vents, cabinets, light fixtures and window tracks. Our post-construction cleaning works from the top down with HEPA vacuums and microfiber, clearing fine dust from every surface before detailing floors, glass and fixtures.

We work with homeowners after a remodel as well as builders and contractors who need a space ready for a final walkthrough or handoff. We can schedule a rough clean during the project and a final clean at the finish.

FAQ

Post-Construction Cleaning questions

When should post-construction cleaning be scheduled?

Once trades are finished and the space is safe to access. For larger projects, we often do a rough clean near the end of construction and a final clean right before the walkthrough.

Do you remove large debris or construction materials?

We clear dust, small debris and residue. Large materials, dumpsters and hauling should be handled by your contractor before we arrive.

Do you work directly with contractors?

Yes. We regularly coordinate with builders, contractors and property managers on timing, access and final walkthroughs.
