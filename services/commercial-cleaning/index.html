Your commercial cleaning checklist

A clear look at what our team takes care of. Have special requests? Add them to your quote request.

Workspaces

  • Desks and work surfaces wiped
  • Phones, keyboards and touch points disinfected
  • Trash and recycling emptied
  • Conference rooms reset
  • Glass partitions and doors cleaned
  • Carpets vacuumed

Restrooms and Break Rooms

FAQ

Commercial Cleaning questions

Can you clean after business hours?

Yes. Most of our commercial clients prefer early morning, evening or weekend service so cleaning never interrupts the workday.

How often should an office be cleaned?

It depends on size and foot traffic. Many offices choose one to three visits per week, while busier spaces may need daily service. We will recommend a schedule after a walkthrough.

Do you offer a walkthrough before quoting?

Yes. For commercial spaces we prefer a quick walkthrough so we can understand the layout, priorities and access before giving an accurate quote.
