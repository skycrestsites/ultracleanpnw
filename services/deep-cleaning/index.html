Deep Cleaning

Deep cleaning for the details everyone else misses

When a home needs more than a surface clean, our deep cleaning goes after built-up grime, soap scum and dust in the places that are easy to overlook. It is the ideal first visit and a great seasonal reset.

Overview

What to expect

A deep clean includes everything in our residential service, plus detailed work that brings a home back to a true baseline. We hand wipe baseboards and door frames, scrub grout, descale fixtures, degrease the kitchen and clear dust from vents, blinds and high ledges.

We recommend a deep clean for a first visit, after a busy season, before or after hosting, or any time the home needs a full reset. Many clients follow it with recurring cleaning to keep that level of clean in place.

FAQ

Deep Cleaning questions

What is the difference between a standard clean and a deep clean?

A standard clean maintains a home that is already in good shape. A deep clean adds detailed tasks like baseboards, grout, fixtures, vents and built-up grime, so it takes longer and covers more ground.

How often should I schedule a deep clean?

Most households benefit from a deep clean two to four times a year, or as a first visit before starting a recurring plan.

Can I add inside the oven or fridge?

Yes. Inside the oven, inside the fridge and interior windows can be added to any deep clean. Just mention them when you request your quote.
