Recurring cleaning that keeps your home ready every day

Overview

What to expect

Recurring cleaning covers all of the essentials on every visit: kitchens, bathrooms, dusting and floors. Because we visit regularly, we also rotate through detail tasks over time so nothing builds up between appointments.

Choose weekly, every two weeks or monthly, and keep the same day and time slot whenever possible. Need to skip or move a visit for a trip or a holiday? Just let us know ahead of time.

FAQ

Recurring Cleaning questions

Do I need a deep clean before starting a recurring plan?

If the home has not been professionally cleaned recently, we usually recommend starting with a deep clean. It sets a strong baseline so every recurring visit can focus on upkeep.

Can I change my schedule later?

Yes. You can switch between weekly, bi-weekly and monthly, or pause service, with notice ahead of your next visit.

Will I have the same cleaner each time?

We aim to keep a consistent team on your home so they learn your preferences, though staffing can occasionally vary.
