Move-in and move-out cleaning for a smooth handoff

Moving is a lot of work already. We clean the empty home from the inside of every cabinet to the last baseboard, so it is ready for a walkthrough, a new tenant or your first night in a new place.

Overview

What to expect

An empty home shows everything. Our move cleaning focuses on the areas landlords, property managers and buyers check most: inside cabinets and drawers, inside appliances, closets, baseboards, window tracks and every floor.

We work with renters hoping to leave a rental in great shape, homeowners preparing to list or hand over keys, and families who want a fresh, clean start before unpacking. Schedule as soon as the home is empty for the best results.

Your move-in & move-out cleaning checklist

Kitchen

  • Inside all cabinets and drawers
  • Inside the refrigerator and freezer
  • Inside the oven and microwave
  • Countertops, sink and backsplash

FAQ

Move-In & Move-Out Cleaning questions

Does the home need to be empty?

Yes, for the best results. An empty home lets us reach inside cabinets, closets and along every wall. If a few items remain, let us know when booking.

Will this help me get my deposit back?

Our move-out clean targets the areas that are checked during walkthroughs. Final deposit decisions are up to your landlord, but a professional clean gives you the best possible start.

Do you work with property managers?

Yes. We can coordinate access and timing with landlords, property managers and real estate agents for turnovers between tenants or before a listing.
