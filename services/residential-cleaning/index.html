Your residential cleaning checklist

A clear look at what our team takes care of. Have special requests? Add them to your quote request.

Kitchen

  • Countertops and backsplash wiped and sanitized
  • Sink and faucet scrubbed and polished
  • Stovetop and appliance exteriors cleaned

FAQ

Residential Cleaning questions

How long does a standard house cleaning take?

Most homes take between two and four hours depending on size, condition and the number of rooms. We will give you a time estimate along with your quote.

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

No. Our team arrives with professional supplies and equipment. If you prefer certain products in your home, let us know and we are happy to use them.

Can you clean around pets?

Yes. Many of the homes we clean have dogs and cats. Just let us know about any pets ahead of time so we can plan around them.
