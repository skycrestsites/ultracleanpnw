FAQ

Questions we hear often

What areas do you serve?

We serve Vancouver, WA and surrounding Clark County communities like Camas, Washougal, Battle Ground and Ridgefield, plus Portland, OR and nearby cities including Beaverton, Hillsboro, Lake Oswego, Tigard and Gresham. If you are close by and do not see your city listed, reach out and ask.

How do I get a quote?

Fill out the quote form or send us an email with your address, the size of your space and the service you need. We will follow up with a clear quote, usually within one business day.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No. Many clients provide a door code, lockbox or key. Whatever works best for you, we will make sure your home is secured when we leave.

Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?

Yes. Our team arrives with professional supplies and equipment. If you prefer specific products, let us know and we are happy to use them.

What is the difference between a standard clean and a deep clean?

A standard clean maintains a home that is already in good shape. A deep clean adds detailed work like baseboards, grout, fixtures, vents and built-up grime. We usually recommend a deep clean for a first visit.

Do you clean both homes and businesses?

Yes. We clean houses, apartments, rentals and move-outs, as well as offices and commercial spaces on both sides of the Columbia River.
